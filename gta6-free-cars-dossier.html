GTA 6 — Free Cars Dossier

Every supercar (and notable non-super) you can take without buying it, with where it sits, what you need to steal it, what tracker it carries, and what it costs to make it yours.

About "Paul Walker's Supra": the tribute car in the game is not a Supra. It is a green Maibatsu '97 Penumbra FF, modelled on the 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse from the first Fast and the Furious. It is the first card below.

How trackers decide what you can keep

NO TRACKER

Take it to Moses at Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn, after his Chapter 2 mission. Your first registration with him is free, so use it on the most expensive tracker-free car you can find. He has a short cooldown and refuses cars stolen close to his garage.

STANDARD TRACKER

Shown as a yellow light. Blocks mod shops and sales until you pay to register the car at a Pay 'n' Spray, which opens in Chapter 3. Whether it also calls the police is not confirmed.

ADVANCED TRACKER

Shown as a red light. Reports your position to police whenever you slow down or stop. Pay 'n' Spray refuses these and the tracker cannot be removed. One report (Forbes) says Randy will take them later in the story; other guides say they can never be registered.

Tools

Slim jim for older cars. Key Cloner for modern exotics: story-locked until about halfway through Chapter 3 (mission Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs).

Fees

"Free" means free to take. After the first Moses freebie, registering costs money: roughly $4,000–$10,000 for ordinary fast cars, and $28,000–$66,000 reported for rare standout supercars.

Insurance

I found no guide describing an insurance system in GTA 6. Registration is what makes a car permanent and storable. Treat insurance as unknown for now.

Suggested order

1. Free roam start: grab the Penumbra FF on Vespucci Island and keep it safe.
2. Chapter 2, after Moses: spend the free registration on the most valuable tracker-free car you have (the Penumbra FF is the usual pick).
3. Chapter 3: Pay 'n' Spray opens for standard-tracker cars; do the Chatfield robbery for the Purple Drank Emerus.
4. Mid Chapter 3: after Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs you have the Key Cloner, which opens the Infernus, Vitello, Furia, Tempesta and Itali RSX.
